## “The redesign gives us 85% margin”

**Answer:** October Lumen's project-review margin calculates to **62.5%**, under
the supplied company definition. This does not measure redesign causation or net profit.

**Why:** The [reconciled ledger](sources/tables/ledger-reconciled.csv), MS/Lumen/2026-10
row, records USD4,000 recognized fees and USD1,500 posted direct cost:
`(4,000 − 1,500) / 4,000 = 62.5%`.
The [owner definition](sources/owner/definitions-and-authority.md#metric) excludes
sales tax and unposted owner-time estimates. [Invoice I-71](sources/tables/10-invoices.csv)
is USD4,500 gross including USD500 tax; it is issued, not recorded as collected.
The Expansion invoice is a draft. Neither changes this calculation's denominator.

The [original ledger](sources/tables/09-ledger.csv) recorded USD2,000 cost, implying
50% under the same definition. The [working note](sources/notes/13-close-query.md)
only suspected a duplicate. The [November 2 correction](sources/owner/2026-11-02-correction.md)
records the posted USD500 reversal. This is a reconciliation of October costs,
not a measured redesign benefit. [Interview feedback](sources/notes/18-feedback.md)
contains no profitability study.

## Why three summaries don't make three confirmations

The [sales statement](sources/chat/03-sales.txt) became the [team note](sources/notes/04-team.md)
and then the [board summary](sources/notes/05-board.md). The
[new sales summary](sources/notes/2026-11-06-summary.md) copies that board summary
without inspecting the finance correction or QA forecast. Preserve that lineage;
repetition adds no independent evidence for the rate, profit or completion claims.
